The problem

Your plan is made at HQ.
Growth is won on five million shelves.

Between the two, three people decide what actually sells, every single day. Today, each of them decides without the full picture.

  1. The distributordecides how much to carry

    Orders on instinct and last month’s numbers. Holds back while claims are pending. Fast lines sell out by midweek.

  2. The salespersondecides where your growth goes

    Walks the same route every week. Orders depend on memory. Slipping outlets get missed.

  3. The retailerdecides who earns the shelf

    Orders what the rep remembers to mention. Rarely hears about the latest scheme. Your brand loses the shelf.

And by the time HQ sees it, secondary sales arrive weeks late in spreadsheets. Decisions run on old numbers.
